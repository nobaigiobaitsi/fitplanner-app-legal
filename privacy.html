Privacy Policy

A clear explanation of how FitPlanner handles information.

Effective 23 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FitPlanner, published by Stavros Angelopoulos ("I", "me", or "FitPlanner"), handles information when you use the FitPlanner mobile application or visit these public privacy and support pages.

The short version: FitPlanner does not require an account. Your workout data is stored locally on your device, and I do not operate a server that receives it. The current app does not include advertising, analytics, or cross-app tracking.

1. Information stored by the app

When you use FitPlanner, the app can store the following information locally on your device:

  • workout plans and the day assigned to each workout;
  • selected exercises, sets, repetitions, weight, and rest time;
  • favorite exercises;
  • active workout-session progress; and
  • completed workout history.

This information is used only to provide the app's planning, workout, favorites, and progress features. It is not uploaded to a FitPlanner account or synced to a FitPlanner cloud service because those services do not exist in the current version.

2. Information the app does not collect

The current version of FitPlanner does not:

  • create or require user accounts;
  • collect your name, address, contacts, or precise location;
  • collect health-platform or wearable data;
  • use advertising or analytics SDKs;
  • track you across apps or websites; or
  • sell or share personal information for advertising.

3. Support communications

If you email for support, I receive the email address you use and the information you choose to include in your message. I use that information to investigate the issue, reply to you, and maintain a reasonable record of the support request. Your email provider and my email provider may also process the message under their own privacy policies.

Do not include medical records or other sensitive health information in a support request. You may ask me to delete a support conversation by using the contact details below, subject to any information I must retain for legal or security reasons.

4. Public website hosting

These privacy and support pages are hosted using GitHub Pages. When you visit them, GitHub may process routine technical information such as your IP address, browser or device information, and request logs to operate and secure the service. FitPlanner does not add analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts to these pages. For details, see the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

5. Data retention and deletion

App data remains on your device until you remove it. You can delete it at any time from Settings → Delete all data in FitPlanner. Uninstalling the app will normally remove its local data, subject to your operating system and device-backup settings.

Support emails are kept only as long as reasonably necessary to answer the request, address follow-up issues, and meet legitimate legal or security needs.

6. Security

FitPlanner limits data handling by keeping workout information on the device. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, so you should protect access to your device with the security features available on it.

7. Children's privacy

FitPlanner is not directed to children under 13. If you are under the age required to consent to digital services where you live, use the app only with permission and supervision from a parent or guardian.

8. Health and exercise information

FitPlanner is a planning and logging tool, not a medical device. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Exercise involves risk; follow the in-app Health & Exercise Disclaimer and seek appropriate professional advice before beginning or changing an exercise program.

9. Changes to this policy

I may update this policy if FitPlanner's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when a new version is published. Material changes will be reflected in the app or on this page before they take effect where required.

10. Contact

FitPlanner is owned and published by Stavros Angelopoulos. For privacy questions or requests, email steveangelopoulos@gmail.com.
