Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. FitPlanner does not require an account. Workout plans, favorites, active sessions, and workout history are stored locally on your device.

How do I save a workout plan?

Changes to your workout are saved automatically as you edit it. Tap Done to return to the selected day in your weekly planner. You do not need to start the workout to save it.

Does FitPlanner back up or sync my data?

No. The current version has no FitPlanner cloud backup or cross-device sync. Deleting the app, clearing its storage, or losing the device may remove your data permanently.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings in FitPlanner and tap Delete all data. Confirming this removes workouts, favorites, active sessions, and workout history stored by the app on that device. This cannot be undone.

The app is not working as expected. What should I try?

  1. Close FitPlanner completely and open it again.
  2. Restart your device.
  3. Install the latest FitPlanner version from the app store.
  4. If the problem continues, email the developer with the details listed above and a screenshot if it does not expose private data.

Is FitPlanner medical advice?

No. FitPlanner is a planning and logging tool, not a medical device. Stop exercising and seek appropriate professional help if you feel pain, dizziness, faintness, unusual shortness of breath, or other concerning symptoms.
